# ReqHub

## Project Description

ReqHub is a web-based reverse marketplace platform that connects users with nearby service providers through a buyer-first approach. Instead of searching through multiple listings, users simply post what they need, and relevant providers respond with competitive quotes. The platform is designed to make finding products and services faster, especially for urgent, location-based, or highly specific requests.

---

## Problem Statement

Current online marketplaces are primarily seller-driven, requiring users to search through numerous listings to find suitable products or services. This approach can be time-consuming and inefficient for urgent, location-based, or highly specific needs. There is a need for a platform where users can simply post their requirements and receive offers from relevant providers, reducing search effort and improving response time.

---

## Objectives

"To develop a web-based reverse marketplace where users post requirements and receive competitive offers from verified nearby providers, reducing search time and improving response speed for urgent or specific needs."

---
